## About

BNSP-Certified Data Analyst, trained on Indonesia's official SKKNI Data Analyst competency curriculum (PPKD Jakarta Selatan, Batch II: 360 hours, 93.45/100 assignment average). 12 months of operational research and public policy experience at **Bank Indonesia** and the **House of Representatives (DPR RI)**.
